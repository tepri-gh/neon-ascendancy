## 1. Summary

The Mox are Night City's only **protection gang**: ≈200–250 members in 2077,
threat level "Low", formed on 13 May 2067 out of the riots after Tyger Claws
murdered the brothel owner Elizabeth "Lizzie" Borden. They describe themselves
as "those who protect working girls and guys". `[wiki]` The wiki's organization
infobox gives `headquarters = Lizzie's Bar`,
`location = Watson, Westbrook`, and `faction_locations = Jig-Jig Street,
Cherry Blossom Market`. `[wiki]`

Their economic model is **a profession, not a district**:

- **Braindance club** — "Most of the Moxes' income was generated by Lizzie's
  Bar, which was an exclusive braindance club." `[wiki]`
- **Sex work** — they "run several smaller brothels where they profit from
  prostitution", and they "demanded substantial fee" from the sex workers they
  protect. `[wiki]`
- **Protection** — the fee itself: they protect sex workers "who operated in
  the vicinity of Lizzie's Bar and their other establishments". `[wiki]`
- **Clinics** — they run clinics alongside the brothels. `[wiki]`
- **Retail / apparel** — the BITCH V.13 clothing brand is managed by the Mox and
  sold through cheap stores and street vendors. `[wiki]`
- **Vehicles / autoshop** — Autowerks, a Mox autoshop and warehouse in
  Northside. `[wiki]` `[game-data]`

The gang is **tiny in the extract**: 1 roster site, 3 units, 1 seeded
sub-territory, 20 community areas, 15 spawn points. They are also the only gang
V cannot attack under normal circumstances. `[wiki]`

The important structural fact, and the one this document is built around:
**the Mox's footprint is a building plus a profession, not turf.** The 2077
artbook says "the few blocks around this establishment is the only 'turf' they
control beyond their dollhouses and clinics." `[wiki]` Their sub-district
footprint is therefore not a map of territory but a list of **establishments**:
Lizzie's Bar (Kabuki, marker-only), a Little China sex shop with Mox workspots,
Autowerks (Northside), and a handful of ambient Mox NPCs elsewhere.

### 3.1 How the name→coordinate links were proven

Only two of the three site-backed points needed real proof; the rest are
area-node coordinates.

- **Lizzie's Bar** has **no `sites.json` row, no marker family of its own, and
  no community in `communities.json`** — §1.1 item 3's "main-quest interiors
  usually have no `sites.json` row" applies exactly. It is proven by:
  1. the raw sector prefab path `$/03_night_city/c_watson/kabuki/loc_q004_lizzys_nightclub_prefabMARLNHA/`
     with sub-prefabs `#loc_q004_lizzys_nightclub_gameplay/#loc_q004_lizzys_nightclub_devices/`

  2. the interior prefab `#loc_q004_lizzys_nightclub_env/interior_prefabL7KH6RQ/`

  3. the openworld prefab with `#lizzies_club_ws_mox_dj`,
     `#lizzies_club_ws_mox_reception` and six `#spl_lizzies_default_moxes_waitress_ground_*`
     patrol paths — the strongest possible statement that Mox staff work here;
  4. `gamedataDistrict.Kabuki_LizziesBar = 84` (§1.1 item 3: primary evidence);
  5. 10 `areas.jsonl` rows (46 Mox entries) and 34 markers named `*lizzies*`.

  `[game-data]`
- **Autowerks** is proven by the wiki `baseid = sts_wat_nid_01` join (§1.1 item
  1) plus the three Mox roster records on site 407. `[game-data]` `[wiki]`
- **Little China sex shop** is proven by workspot names alone — `_mox_day`,
  `_mox_night_001/002`, `_pimp`, `_client_001`, `_couple_001/002` under
  `#wat_lch_ws_sexshop_01_*`. Note this is a **workspot cluster in the
  `little_china_openworld` sector, not a site**: the prefab is
  `little_china_community_prefabYXM4BXA/#wat_lch_sexshop_01_com`. `[game-data]`

Two coordinate cautions specific to this faction:

- **Marker-only places can be better anchored than sites.** Lizzie's has no
  site row, so the only correct anchor is a marker or area node. Do **not**
  synthesise one from the nearest site (that is exactly what produces the
  Little China mis-attribution).
- **`*_spwn_player_*` markers are teleports.** `q000_corpo_spwn_player_lizzies_club`
  sits at `z = 31.3`, three metres above and behind the lounge — a spawn pad,
  not the club. Use `_front_entrance` / `_club_lounge` / `_sm_lizzies` markers.

## 5. Headquarters recommendation

### Primary HQ: **Lizzie's Bar** — Kabuki, Watson

Coordinates: front entrance `(-1201.905, 1562.259, 24.274)`; interior lounge
`(-1174.420, 1556.280, 23.720)`. `[game-data]`
`markers.jsonl` `q000_corpo_mp_lizzies_front_entrance`, `q000_corpo_04_lizzies_club_lounge`.

Rationale:

1. **It is the canon HQ, stated three times over.** The org infobox reads
   `headquarters = Lizzie's Bar`; the location infobox says "It is also the
   headquarters for the Mox"; the in-game database entry says "The gang's main
   headquarters is Lizzie's Bar". `[wiki]`
2. **It is a real seat with rooms to assign.** The sector prefab gives a
   reception, a main hall, a VIP room (`vip_room_sit`, `vip_couch`, `vip_doors`),
   a bar (`#q105_bartender_clipping_hide`), a basement server room
   (`deco_basement_prefabPGMR34A/vs_secret_room_server_*`) and a roof
   (`roof_door`, `rooftop_door_manual_ctr`). That is a believable org layout:
   front desk, boss's office, vault, server room, escape route. `[game-data]`
3. **It is the income stream.** "The Mox's main source of income" is the club
   itself, so owning the HQ and owning the economy are one action. `[wiki]`
4. **It is the only place with Mox staff on record.** Six waitress patrol paths
   and two Mox workspots (`_ws_mox_dj`, `_ws_mox_reception`) plus 46 Mox entries
   across 10 area rows. Nothing else in the game has that density of Mox bodies.
   `[game-data]`
5. **Independent existence check.** `gamedataDistrict.Kabuki_LizziesBar = 84` —
   the game names the building as its own district enum. `[game-data]`
6. **The seed already nominates Kabuki for the Mox** — it just puts the point in
   the wrong place (§8.1). Retarget, do not invent.

Trade-off: Lizzie's is a **single club on one block**, not a compound. Its
"territory" is the pavement outside, which is precisely the canon situation —
the artbook calls the surrounding few blocks the only turf they hold. Model the
HQ as an enclave inside Tyger Claws-contested Kabuki, not as a district owner.

### Alternate 1: **Autowerks** — Northside

Coordinates `(-1224.176, 2275.871, 6.971)` `[game-data]` site 407.

The better *fortress*: a workshop with a yard, containers, a closed `bbox`, a
gate, a door, a patrol route and `raid_target` suitability, river-adjacent on
Longshore North. It is garrisoned by the only Mox roster units in the game, and
its gig framing ("rescue the BD tuner hiding from an active shooter") means the
building already supports a combat scenario. It loses on canon priority — the
wiki names both Lizzie's Bar and only that as HQ — and on economy: an autoshop
is a front, and the gang's money comes from the club.

### Alternate 2: **Little China sex shop** — LittleChina

Coordinates `(-1523.770, 1783.070, 18.180)` `[game-data]` `{wat_lch_sexshop_01_com}`.

The most *thematically* Mox location in the game and the only place where Mox
workspots are explicitly sex-work workspots (`_mox_day`, `_mox_night_001/002`,
`_pimp`). It is also the only candidate that proves the "smaller brothels"
income line with a game record rather than a book quote. It loses on form: it
is a street-frontage workspot cluster with no interior cell, no site row, no
doors and no guards, so there is nothing to capture but a patch of pavement.

### Overruled / downgraded suggestions

- **`cc_kab_creek_loop` / "Creek Loop Warehouse" as the Mox holding** — the seed's
  only Mox sub-territory. 384.3 m from Lizzie's Bar, 0 Mox area nodes within
  150 m, and its 12 sites are Tyger Claws / Maelstrom / Militech-owned. Retarget
  it, do not keep it. See §8.1.
- **Cherry Blossom Market as a Mox control point** — the wiki's
  `faction_locations` names it, but it is in **JapanTown**, and no site, marker,
  area or node in the extract mentions it in a Mox context. It is a Tyger Claws
  vendor hub with a Tyger pachinko and ripperdoc nearby. `TBD` coordinate, no
  seed. See §8.4.
- **Growl FM / Coastview stand as a Mox point** — an area node with 5 Mox
  entries and a canon Mox member (DJ Ash), but the station is a Dogtown pirate
  radio and the stand is a night-time dance spot. Good flavour, bad control
  point. See §8.5.
- **Jig-Jig Street as a Mox control point** — the wiki lists it as a

- **Any Mox casino, drug lab, chop shop or gun-running point** — no evidence
  exists. The Mox have no `gambling` or `drugs` line in any source. Inventing
  one would mis-model the faction.

### Test control point for the first in-game test: **Autowerks**

Why Autowerks and not the HQ:

- **It is the only site with clean, provable Mox ownership.** `default_faction_id

  whole extract. Eviction is unambiguous.
- **It is enclosed and georeferenced properly.** A site row with a real `bbox`
  (`-1241.5…-1208.5 × 2259.2…2290.4`), a radius of 17.9 m, 17 triggers, 9

  radius without guesswork.

## 7. Per-sub-district count of lore-relevant locations

`named` counts distinct named places the Mox own, run, protect or habitually
occupy — one entry per place, not per quest variant. `filler` is the deduped
occupied-site count minus sites already counted as a named place
(`METHOD.md` §8).

| Sub-district | Named lore-relevant places | Which ones | Mox-occupied sites (deduped, `[game-data]`) | Filler | Noteworthy interiors |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Kabuki | **2** | Lizzie's Bar, Mox parking / `de_wat_kab_10` stand | 0 | 0 | 1 (Lizzie's Bar) |
| Northside | **1** | Autowerks | 1 | 0 | 1 (Autowerks) |
| LittleChina | **1** | Little China sex shop | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| JapanTown | **2** | Jig-Jig Street (beat), Cherry Blossom Market (`[wiki]`, `TBD`) | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Coastview | **1** | Growl FM stand | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| Glen / VistaDelRey / Arroyo | **0** | city-race crowd participation only | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| **Total** | **7** | | 1 | 0 | 2 |

Reconciliation: **1 occupied site** = Autowerks; it is a named place, so
`filler = 0`. **7 named** = 1 site-backed (Autowerks) + 6 marker/area-only
(Lizzie's Bar, Little China sex shop, Mox parking stand, Jig-Jig beat, Cherry
Blossom Market, Growl FM stand). `named + filler = 7`, which does **not**
reconcile against the site count alone — correct for a faction with one site and
six marker-only places (§8 item 17 check).

**Interpretation for yield.** This is the flattest, smallest faction in the set
by an order of magnitude: seven named places across five sub-districts, one
site, zero filler. Three consequences:

1. **Do not give the Mox a district.** They hold a building and a street corner.
   Yield should come from **per-establishment** multipliers
   (`sex_work` + `braindance` + `protection` on Lizzie's), not from territory
   ownership, or a Mox takeover would paint Kabuki in the wrong colour.
2. **Lizzie's Bar must carry most of the faction's income.** Rank 1 is worth
   more than ranks 2–6 combined; the seed's `income_multiplier = 1.0` is a
   reasonable default, but the club should be the single high-yield point.
3. **Two of the seven are `[wiki]`-only with `TBD` coordinates.** Cherry Blossom
   Market and the unnamed Mox clinics should be seeded as *known-but-unplaced*
   (see Gaps), not as control points. That leaves **five seedable points**, of
   which two are genuinely capturable interiors.

## 8. Gaps and uncertainties

1. **The seed's single Mox holding is in the wrong place, and the reason is
   visible in the data.** `cc_kab_creek_loop` (`sub_territory_id 9`) has
   `initial_owner_faction_id = 11`, but 0 Mox area nodes lie within 150 m and
   its 12 `initial_owner == 11` sites are generic `scanner_hustle` blocks whose
   real factions are Tyger Claws (`ma_wat_kab_12`, `ma_wat_nid_04`), Maelstrom
   (`ma_wat_lch_15`, `ma_wat_nid_13`), Militech (`sts_wat_kab_01`) and NCPD
   (`sts_wat_kab_08`). The control centre is 384.3 m from Lizzie's Bar. This is
   the same shape of defect the Tyger Claws doc found from the other side
   (`tyger-claws.md` §8.4: "Kabuki Market's existing CC is Mox-owned… One of
   them is wrong."). **Both docs are describing the same mis-assignment.**
   Kabuki Market is Tyger turf; the Mox's real anchor is Lizzie's Bar.
2. **METHOD §3.3's `nearest-site` fallback splits a single building across two
   sub-districts.** The 9 Lizzie's area rows attribute 5 to LittleChina and 4 to
   Kabuki, because `ma_wat_lch_14` (90.1 m) beats `ma_wat_kab_08` (106.4 m) on
   distance while the centroid test also picks `lch_canal_overpass` (89.7 m).
   The prefab path and `Kabuki_LizziesBar = 84` both say Kabuki. **METHOD should
   add: for marker-only places with no site row, token and prefab attribution
   must precede the nearest-site fallback**, since the nearest site can easily be
   a different district's scanner hustle across a street.
3. **Lizzie's Bar has no `sites.json` row, no `communities.json` entry and no
   dedicated marker family.** It is invisible to §3.1, §3.4 and §3.5 and only
   findable via §3.6 plus raw-sector prefab greps. This is a sharper version of
   `maelstrom.md` §8.3: for the Mox the *headquarters itself* is marker-only.
   The playbook's "check the seed first" step (§3.0) is actively misleading
   here, because the seed's named holding is 384 m from the real place.
4. **Cherry Blossom Market is listed by the wiki but has no game record.** The
   org infobox gives `faction_locations = Jig-Jig Street, Cherry Blossom
   Market`. Cherry Blossom Market's own page places it in **JapanTown**,
   Westbrook, with 2077 vendors Chiyo Omoto (ripperdoc), Christine Markov, Karim's
   Vinyls and a Ramen Joint. No site, marker, area node or raw prefab in the
   extract mentions it in any gang context; the only trace is
   `jpn_bazar_003_ws_d_cherry_blosom_*` workspots under the JapanTown openworld
   sector. Treat the Mox claim as **lore adjacency** (they protect sex workers who
   work the market), not ownership, and leave the coordinate `TBD`. `[wiki]`
5. **The Growl FM stand is a real Mox area but not a Mox business.** `DJ Ash` is
   listed on *Moxes Members* as a member and hosts 89.7 Growl FM, and
   `{growl_stand_comm}` at `(-1882.204, -1852.352)` is 5-Mox-dominant (5 Mox,
   2 Voodoo Boys). But Growl FM is a Dogtown pirate station and the stand is a
   night-time dance spot near Batty's Hotel. Good flavour; do not give it Mox
   revenue.
6. **The Mox "run … clinics" (plural) but not one clinic is identifiable.** The
   wiki says they run "several smaller brothels … as well as clinics", and the
   `Moxes Members` list gives no ripperdoc. I searched every marker for
   `clinic|ripperdoc|medic` and every area for Mox faction counts near Kabuki
   clinics (`kab_05_spawnpoint_ripperdoc`, Vik's, Misty's): none is Mox-flagged.
   `sts_wat_kab_05` is a **Scavenger** gig with `#kab_05_com_1st_floor_prostitutes`
   and a `#kab_05_tr_ripperdoc_clinic` trigger — a brothel-plus-clinic that would
   look like a Mox site at a glance, but `default_faction_id = 7`. That is a real
   trap. Leave Mox clinics as `[wiki]`/`TBD`.
7. **Site 407's `initial_owner_faction_id` is Maelstrom (1), not Mox (11).** It
   follows the seeded sub-territory owner (`nid_martin_st_docks`, Maelstrom), so
   it is not evidence of Mox ownership by itself — §2.2 of METHOD warns exactly
   this. The Mox claim rests on `default_faction_id = 11` plus the roster, which
   is the correct pair.
8. **`is_interior = 0` on Autowerks, yet it is an enclosed building.** Its `bbox`
   is a closed 33 × 31 m block and its node names include `#nid_01_tr_door`,
   `#nid_01_tr_gate`, `#nid_01_tr_window`, `#nid_01_tr_container` and
   `#nid_01_tr_enter_workshop_area`. The flag is sparse (METHOD §2.2); do not
   trust its absence. The JSON block therefore sets `interior: true` on the basis
   of node names, not the flag.
9. **The Mox are the only gang V cannot attack.** This is trivia for the doc but
   a real modelling question for the mod: if the player founds an organisation
   and can capture Mox points, the faction's vanilla non-hostility is being
   overridden by design. Worth a deliberate decision rather than an accident.
10. **No vendor inventory in the extract.** "What each business sells" is always
    `[wiki]`. Lizzie's `raid_target`-only suitability tag says nothing about a
    club; the club-ness comes from prefab names (`vip_room`, `bar`, `dancefloor`,
    `#mirror_plane_lizzies_club`) and the database entry.
11. **The vanilla per-sub-district gang panel was never dumped.** Evidence (b) in
    §2.2 is `data/districts.json` plus the earlier wiki-derived table. Note the
    contradiction it already contains: the district-level panel lists the Mox
    **only under Westbrook**, yet their HQ is in Watson/Kabuki. A TweakDB
    `District_Record.Gangs` dump would settle Kabuki and Northside exactly.
12. **`{sq024_com_city_race}` resolves to Glen by position but is labelled
    `city_center`.** It sits at `y = -343.2`, inside the CityCenter bbox's `y`
    range but outside its `x` range, and its nearest geocoded site is in Glen.
    This is the same class of defect as `maelstrom.md` §8.1; it is a race-crowd
    area, so it does not affect any count that matters.
