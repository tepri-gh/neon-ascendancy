# Neon Ascendancy Glossary

Canonical domain terms used across docs, data seeds, SQLite schemas, and code.
Use these exact names in types, tables, and commits.

## World & Territory

- **District:** A top-level Night City / Badlands / Dogtown region matching
  vanilla `gamedataDistrict` top-level divisions (e.g., Watson, Westbrook,
  Heywood, Santo Domingo, Pacifica, City Center, Badlands, Dogtown).
- **Sub-Territory:** A pre-partitioned geographic subdivision inside a District
  that can be individually owned, contested, and displayed on the World Map.
  Target baseline granularity is ~5 Sub-Territories per major District.
- **Control Center:** The physical anchor point `(x, y, z)` inside a
  Sub-Territory (typically an affiliated gang compound, plaza, warehouse, or
  building) where faction NPCs spawn and which renders as a dot on the map.
- **Node:** *(New model, replacing Sub-Territory/Control Center; see
  [`design/territory-data-model.md`](design/territory-data-model.md).)*
  A place a faction holds, with one controller, a scale (Hangout, Outpost,
  Landmark, Stronghold, Business, HQ) that sets its influence, and a type
  (its pillar focus). In code and tables, `territory_node`, to avoid
  confusion with the engine's world nodes (`NodeRef`).
- **Bloc:** An internal political faction (vassal) inside a faction, with
  its own leader.
- **Title Holder:** The faction with the legal/political claim to a
  Node, which may differ from its controller.
- **Spawn Point:** A ground-truth world coordinate and faction affiliation
  extracted from Cyberpunk 2077 2.5 data, linked to a Sub-Territory.
- **Road Chunk:** *(Deferred)* A discrete spatial partition of the drivable road
  network used to index offscreen vehicle transits.

## Actors & Organizations

- **Faction:** Any NPC power group operating in Night City—categorized as
  **Gang**, **Megacorp**, **Fixer Network**, or **Civic / Independent**
  (e.g., NCPD, Delamain, The Mox, Afterlife Mercs, Trauma Team).
- **Organization (Org):** The player-founded entity in Neon Ascendancy—either a
  **Gang** (starts by seizing a Sub-Territory Control Center) or a
  **Corporation** (requires startup capital and purchasing a physical asset).
- **Notoriety / Heat:** Numeric pressure generated by raids, territory flips,
  and overt violence. Drives faction retaliation, NCPD pressure, mission
  availability, and market volatility.

## Simulation & Missions

- **Cycle:** One fixed in-game `GameTime` simulation interval (e.g., in-game day
  or multi-hour period) on which periodic territory income, upkeep, and world
  reactions settle deterministically.
- **Dynamic Mission:** A contract or operation generated from current world
  state rather than static story quests—categorized as **Combat / Military**,
  **Economic**, or **Espionage**.
- **SimCommand:** An external input translated by a thin redscript adapter into
  the simulation core (e.g., `FoundGang`, `ClearTargetEliminated`, `AdvanceTime`).
- **SimEvent:** A typed domain fact emitted by one simulation pillar and routed
  through the bounded causal event bus to other pillars (e.g.,
  `MissionCompleted`, `TerritoryOwnerChanged`, `IncomeSettled`).
- **Transit Ledger:** *(Deferred)* Indexed SQLite schedule of offscreen vehicles
  traveling between points A and B across Road Chunks.

## Sandbox & Persistence

- **World Era:** The detected vanilla story state of a save when Neon Ascendancy
  is started (e.g., pre-Heist Act 1, post-Heist Act 2+, Phantom Liberty
  standalone, or third-party skip-intro save).
- **Sandbox Sanitizer:** The idempotent redscript subsystem that lifts city
  lockdowns, disables Johnny, and suppresses vanilla quests, gigs, fixer calls,
  and quest dialogue while preserving working shops and open-world systems.
- **Save Generation:** Monotonic per-playthrough integer persisted in the tiny
  redscript `ScriptableSystem` anchor and matched to SQLite checkpoints so
  reloading older saves restores exact corresponding database state.
- **Schema Version (`SCHEMA_VERSION`):** Monotonic integer tracking persisted
  save/database layout compatibility (see `docs/versioning.md`).
